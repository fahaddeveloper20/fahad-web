Return & Refund Policy

Our commitment to quality and client satisfaction. Learn about our refund eligibility criteria and process.

Last Updated: December 2025

Our Commitment

At Muhammad Fahad Web Development, we are committed to delivering high-quality web development services that meet and exceed client expectations. This Return & Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued and the process for requesting them.

We believe in transparent communication and fair business practices. Please read this policy carefully before engaging our services.

Important Notice

Due to the nature of custom web development services, refunds are only available under specific circumstances outlined in this policy. Digital services cannot be "returned" like physical products.

Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be considered under the following circumstances:

Eligible for Refund

Situation Eligibility Notes
Project cancellation before work begins Full Refund 100% of deposit refunded
Failure to deliver agreed services Full Refund After formal complaint resolution
Significant deviation from agreed specifications Partial Refund Case-by-case assessment
Technical issues that cannot be resolved Partial Refund After 14 days of troubleshooting

Not Eligible for Refund

Situation Status Reason
Change of mind after project commencement Not Eligible Work already completed
Delays caused by client Not Eligible Missing content/feedback
Third-party service issues Not Eligible Outside our control
Minor aesthetic preferences Not Eligible Subjective preferences

Refund Request Process

If you believe you are eligible for a refund, follow this process:

1

Contact Us

Send an email to fahad.developer3122@gmail.com with "Refund Request" in the subject line. Include your project details and reason for refund request.

2

Review Period

We will review your request within 3-5 business days. We may request additional information or schedule a call to discuss the situation.

3

Resolution Discussion

We will discuss possible resolutions, which may include project revisions, additional work, or a partial/full refund if eligible.

4

Processing

If approved, refunds are processed within 7-14 business days to the original payment method. You will receive confirmation via email.

Cancellation Policy

Projects may be cancelled under the following terms:

Client-Initiated Cancellation

Before Work Begins
Full refund of any deposits paid
During Development Phase
Refund proportional to work not completed, minus 20% administrative fee
After Delivery
No refund available - maintenance phase begins

Developer-Initiated Cancellation

We reserve the right to cancel a project and issue a full refund in exceptional circumstances, such as:

  • Client becomes unresponsive for more than 30 days
  • Scope changes making project unfeasible
  • Legal or ethical concerns
  • Non-payment of agreed fees

Dispute Resolution

We aim to resolve all issues amicably through the following process:

  1. Informal Resolution: Direct communication to understand and resolve the issue
  2. Formal Complaint: Written complaint with detailed explanation
  3. Mediation: Third-party mediation if needed (fees may apply)
  4. Final Decision: Our decision is final after mediation attempts

All disputes shall be governed by the laws of Pakistan. Any legal proceedings shall take place in Karachi, Pakistan.

Revisions & Fixes Policy

Before considering a refund, we offer:

Included Revisions

All projects include:

  • 3 rounds of design revisions
  • Unlimited bug fixes for 30 days post-launch
  • Minor content updates for 14 days
  • Technical support for 30 days

Additional Work

If additional work is needed beyond the agreed scope:

  • We will provide a quote for additional work
  • Work begins after quote approval
  • Additional work may prevent refund eligibility

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the refund process take?

+

Once approved, refunds are processed within 7-14 business days. The time it takes for the refund to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment processor.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied with the design?

+

We include design revisions in all projects. If you're not satisfied, we'll work with you through the revision process. Refunds for subjective design preferences are generally not eligible, but we're committed to finding a solution that meets your needs.

What if the website has technical issues?

+

All projects include 30 days of technical support and bug fixes. If we're unable to resolve technical issues within a reasonable timeframe, a partial refund may be considered based on the situation.

Do you offer money-back guarantees?

+

We don't offer blanket money-back guarantees due to the custom nature of our work. However, we stand behind our work and offer refunds in situations where we fail to deliver as agreed in the project contract.

What payment methods do you accept for refunds?

+

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the transaction. We accept and refund through bank transfer, PayPal, and other agreed-upon payment methods.

Need Help with a Refund Request?

If you have questions about our refund policy or need to initiate a refund request, please contact us directly. We're here to help resolve any issues.
